About Bricks by the Bag

Bricks by the Bag is a small, collector-friendly storefront built for fans who love iconic LEGO® sets. We focus on premium builds, clean product pages, and a smooth checkout experience.

What we care about

  • Collector-ready shipping (extra protection on big boxes)
  • Clear product info with photos, details, and reviews
  • Fast browsing with Quick View + full product pages

Shipping & returns

Orders typically ship within 1–2 business days. Returns are accepted within 14 days as long as items are unused and in original packaging.
